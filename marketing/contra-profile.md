## Rate

Contra asks for an hourly figure even though you sell fixed-price. Put one in — a blank
rate reads as "hasn't finished setting up."

Back-solve it from what you already charge: a $800 Site Sprint delivered in ~5 working days
is roughly **$20–25/hr**. That's low for AU/NZ/US buyers and high for the PH market, which
is exactly the position you want on a marketplace — competitive on price, credible on
portfolio.

Then **always steer to fixed price in the proposal.** It's your actual differentiator and it
protects you from scope creep.
